One Small Precision to Add
"Uneven logs" is close but the exact word is: SPIKE or BURST

Normal:  occasional WARN, spaced far apart in time
         (like 1 every few minutes — background noise)

Anomaly: many WARNs clustered close together in time
         (like 5+ within 5 seconds — signals real trouble)

The detector doesn't care about odd/uneven PATTERNS —
it specifically cares about RATE — how many events per
unit of time. That's the technical term: rate-based
anomaly detection.

The Analogy — Sliding Window Explained
Imagine you're a security guard watching a door.
Your rule: "If more than 5 people walk in within
any 5-second window, sound the alarm."

You don't count ALL people who ever walked in (that's
just a total count). You only care about a RECENT
WINDOW of time — the last 5 seconds, continuously sliding
forward as time passes.

person walks in at 0:00 → window: [0:00]
person walks in at 0:01 → window: [0:00, 0:01]
person walks in at 0:02 → window: [0:00, 0:01, 0:02]
person walks in at 0:03 → window: [0:00, 0:01, 0:02, 0:03]
person walks in at 0:04 → window: [0:00, 0:01, 0:02, 0:03, 0:04]
                           → 5 people in 5 seconds → ALARM!
                           → clear the window, start counting again
Why 80 WARNs Became 16 Anomalies (Not 80 Anomalies)
Your 80 WARN messages were sent very fast (0.05s delay each)
80 warnings × 0.05s = only 4 seconds total to send them all

Since they arrived in clusters within the same 5-second
windows, the detector fired an alert every time it accumulated
5 warnings, then cleared and started counting again:

Warnings 1-5   → 5 in window → ANOMALY #1 → clear
Warnings 6-10  → 5 in window → ANOMALY #2 → clear
Warnings 11-15 → 5 in window → ANOMALY #3 → clear
...continues...
80 warnings ÷ 5 per anomaly = 16 anomalies exactly
That's exactly why you got 16 — it's mathematically precise: 80 ÷ 5 = 16.

Why Answer Relevancy Is Low (0.304) — Real Diagnosis
RAGAS's answer_relevancy metric works by:
1. Take your answer
2. Ask the judge LLM to generate 3 questions THAT answer would address
3. Embed those generated questions
4. Compare to the ORIGINAL question (which in our case = the raw log trigger)
The problem: your "question" is a raw log line, not a natural language question:
question = "10.251.199.19:50010:Got exception while serving 
            blk_8466246428293623262 to /10.251.106.37:"
This is NOT phrased as a question. RAGAS generates natural-language questions from your answer ("What caused the DataNode exception?") and compares them to this raw log string — which will have naturally low semantic similarity, since a log line and a question are structurally very different text types, even when topically related.
This is a metric-task mismatch, not a system flaw.
answer_relevancy was designed for: "What is the capital of France?"
                                    → "Paris is the capital of France"
Not for:                           raw log line → diagnosis report
